## City index: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0)

The home-city search (`public/cities.json`) is derived from the
[GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org) `cities15000` dump, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0).

**This is a modified version.** The data was changed as follows (see `scripts/build-cities.ts` and
`scripts/lib/cities.ts`):

- filtered to populated places (feature class P) with a population of at least 50,000;
- sections of populated places (feature code PPLX) excluded;
- coordinates rounded to 3 decimals, population rounded to thousands;
- region names joined from GeoNames' `admin1CodesASCII`, country names generated from country codes;
- reformatted into a compact JSON array layout.

GeoNames does not endorse this work, and the GeoNames data is provided "as is" without warranty.

## Geocoding: OpenStreetMap / Nominatim (ODbL 1.0)

Venue coordinates in `data/geocache.json` are results of the
[Nominatim](https://nominatim.org) geocoder, which serves OpenStreetMap data.
Contains information from OpenStreetMap, © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright),
available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

`data/geocache.json` is a derived database of those results (queries and the coordinates and place
names returned). It is made available under the same ODbL 1.0: you may share and adapt it, provided
you attribute OpenStreetMap contributors, keep it under the ODbL and keep it open.

## Basemap: OpenFreeMap and OpenMapTiles

The map tiles and style are served by [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org), which uses
[OpenMapTiles](https://openmaptiles.org) (schema, © OpenMapTiles, CC BY 4.0) and data ©
[OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) (ODbL). Nothing is bundled:
tiles are fetched by the viewer's browser at runtime. The map's attribution control shows these
credits.

## Emissions factors: UK DESNZ (OGL v3.0)

The CO₂ estimates use the UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ) and DEFRA
[greenhouse gas reporting conversion factors 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greenhouse-gas-reporting-conversion-factors-2026),
stored in `data/co2-factors.json`. Contains public sector information licensed under the
[Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
